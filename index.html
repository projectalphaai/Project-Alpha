About Project Alpha

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

How does the AI content generation work?

Our AI analyzes your brand voice, past content, and audience engagement patterns to generate captions, hashtags, and content ideas that match your style. You can always edit before publishing.

Which social platforms are supported?

Project Alpha supports Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and YouTube out of the box — with more channels added based on customer demand.

Can I switch plans or cancel anytime?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime from your dashboard. Annual plans include a 20% discount and a 14-day refund window.

Is my data secure?

We use enterprise-grade encryption, SOC 2–aligned infrastructure, and never sell your data. OAuth means we never store your social passwords.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes — start a 14-day free trial on any paid plan. No credit card required to explore the platform.

Privacy Policy

Project Alpha AI respects your privacy. We collect only the data necessary to provide our social media automation services. Your OAuth credentials are never stored — we use secure token-based authentication. Analytics data is encrypted at rest and in transit. You may request data deletion at any time by contacting support@projectalpha.ai.

Terms of Service

By using Project Alpha AI, you agree to our terms of service. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled. You retain ownership of all content you create. We reserve the right to suspend accounts that violate platform policies or engage in spam. For enterprise agreements, contact our sales team.
