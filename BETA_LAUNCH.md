# Beta Launch Checklist — Project Alpha (Sprint 8)

Lean beta for first design partners → first paying customer.

## Before inviting anyone
- [ ] Production Readiness Checklist complete for Meta publish
- [ ] Landing pricing CTAs match what you can deliver (IG + FB publish + AI + CRM)
- [ ] Hide or clearly label unfinished areas (Agents / Analytics / Inbox remain gated)
- [ ] Support channel ready (email or shared inbox)
- [ ] Loom of happy path: signup → connect → generate → schedule → publish → CRM lead

## Beta cohort (n=3)
- [ ] Partner 1 invited
- [ ] Partner 2 invited
- [ ] Partner 3 invited
- [ ] Each partner completed: connect + 1 live publish
- [ ] Feedback notes captured (activation blockers only)

## First invoice
- [ ] Price confirmed (Genesis $49 / Ascension $149 or founder deal)
- [ ] Payment collected (Stripe or manual)
- [ ] Access confirmed on production
- [ ] 14-day check-in scheduled

## Kill criteria (pause beta)
- Live publish success rate &lt; 90% over 20 posts
- OAuth connect failure for &gt;30% of attempts
- Data loss or token leak incident

## Do not build during beta
- Extra social networks beyond IG/FB
- AI Agents product
- Social Inbox
- Deep analytics
- Multi-seat RBAC
